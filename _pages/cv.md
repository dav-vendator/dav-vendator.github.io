## Research Summary

<div class="cv-card highlight">
My research explores how quantum systems can represent memory, temporal correlations, and evolving structure through coherent dynamics at the intersection of <strong>Quantum Information Theory</strong>, <strong>Quantum Machine Learning</strong>, and <strong>sequential learning systems</strong>. Coming from a computer science background, I bridge ideas from recurrent architectures, temporal representation learning, and information theory with quantum mechanics and computation.
</div>
