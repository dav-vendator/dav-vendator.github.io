## Current Research Focus

<p class="section-lead">
  Investigating the mechanics of coherent quantum recurrent channels and the emergence of structural memory in non-Markovian quantum evolutions.
</p>

<div class="research-bento">
  <div class="bento-card long">
    <div class="bento-icon">⚛️</div>
    <h3>Coherent Temporal Paradigms</h3>
    <p>A primary axis of my work probes whether multi-particle entanglement and unitary evolution can implicitly maintain long-range dependencies—completely bypassing the destructive decoherence introduced by intermediate measurement steps.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="bento-card">
    <div class="bento-icon">🧠</div>
    <h3>Quantum Memory Profiles</h3>
    <p>Mapping out how information-theoretic bottlenecks constrain memory capacities inside parameterized quantum state tensors across discrete temporal sequences.</p>
  </div>

  <div class="bento-card">
    <div class="bento-icon">🌀</div>
    <h3>Deferred Measurements</h3>
    <p>Developing systemic methodologies to shift state collapses to the terminal boundary of temporal processing pipelines, preserving complex correlation phases.</p>
  </div>
</div>
